Editing. Press E and type: it plays after a short pause, ⌘↵ plays it now. ⌘S saves to src/songs/ under npm run dev, or in this browser on the site, where edits are also kept as you type (revert brings the original back). share copies a link to the song; download saves the .ts file.

Finding things. This sheet, the library (B) and the palette (⌘K) play a sound or an example with ▶ and insert it at the cursor (switching to editing first). + track on the mixer adds a track to the song from a snippet.

Click a section to jump there on the next bar. Or edit src/songs/*.ts in your editor; every save hot-swaps without stopping the music.
